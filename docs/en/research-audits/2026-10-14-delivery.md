## Verification rules

- Executable paths need algorithm behavior tests. Never populate actual results from paper numbers.
- New CUDA paths need real NVIDIA runs and sanitized receipts. Do not fabricate GPU evidence for CPU paths.
- Deferred items do not enter executable adapters/evolve or show unmeasured results on the public dashboard.
- One PR follows completion of scope, catalogs, minimal install, quality checks, and docs build.
- Review watermark stays 2026-10-12 for this delivery. Implementations do not substitute for outstanding new-window coverage.
